NutriFit

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

1. Who is responsible for your data

Ringo Boscan, based in Mexico City, Mexico, is responsible for the personal data you provide when you use the NutriFit app. Contact: support.nutrifit@gmail.com.

2. What data we collect

Type of dataExamples
Account dataName, email address and password (encrypted).
Data to calculate your goalsDate of birth, sex, weight, height, activity level and goal.
Health and food data (sensitive)What you eat and drink, weight history, target weight and dietary restrictions (for example: vegetarian, gluten-free, low sugar, allergies, or anything you type in "Other restrictions").
PhotosFood photos you take to analyze them and, if you add one, your profile photo.
MessagesWhat you write to the AI assistant and the messages you send in "Help & feedback", along with the app version and device type.
AI usageHow many times you used the AI each day (to apply the fair-use limit). To know when your day starts, the app sends your phone's time zone; we don't store it.

We do not collect your location, your contacts or payment data inside the app.

3. How we use your data

  • To create and maintain your account.
  • To calculate your calorie and nutrient goals and log what you eat.
  • To analyze food photos and descriptions to estimate their nutrition values.
  • To suggest meals that respect your restrictions.
  • To show your progress and send you reminders (if you turn them on).
  • To apply the daily AI usage limit.
  • To answer your questions, reports and suggestions, and to improve the app.

We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

4. Sensitive data and your consent

Information about your diet, weight and dietary restrictions may be considered sensitive health-related data. We only use it for the purposes above and with your express consent, which you give by checking the consent box when you create your account. You can withdraw it at any time by deleting your account.

5. Who we share your data with

Only with providers we need for the app to work:

  • Supabase: stores your account and data. Its servers may be located outside your country.
  • Anthropic: processes food descriptions and photos, and messages to the assistant, to generate the analysis or answer. Food photos are not stored by NutriFit after they are analyzed. See Anthropic's privacy policy.
  • Open Food Facts: when you scan a barcode or search for a product, only that code or text is looked up in their public database.
  • Apple and Google: distribute the app through the App Store, TestFlight and Google Play.

We may also share data when required by a competent authority under applicable law.

Profile photo: it is stored at a hard-to-guess link. Nobody can list other users' photos and we don't publish your photo anywhere, but anyone who has that exact link could see it.

6. How long we keep your data

For as long as you have your account. If you delete it, we delete your account and data right away. Our providers' technical backups may be kept for a limited time before they are deleted automatically.

7. Your rights

You can access, correct or delete your data, object to its use and withdraw your consent:

  • In the app: edit your data in Profile or use Profile → Delete my account and data.
  • By email to support.nutrifit@gmail.com, from the address you signed up with, describing your request. We will reply within the time required by applicable law.

Depending on where you live (for example, some U.S. states), you may have additional privacy rights; you can exercise them the same way.

8. Security

We use encrypted connections and access controls so that each user can only see and change their own information. No system is 100% secure, but we work to protect your data.

9. Children

NutriFit is not intended for anyone under 18. You must be 18 or older to create an account.

10. The app is not medical care

Nutrition values are estimates. NutriFit does not provide medical diagnosis or treatment.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, if the change is important, we will let you know in the app.

If there is any difference between language versions of this policy, the Spanish version prevails.
