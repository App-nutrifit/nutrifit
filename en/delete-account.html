Delete your account and data

You can delete your NutriFit account at any time, from the app or without having it installed.

Option 1 · From the app
Go to Profile → Delete my account and data and confirm. Deletion is immediate.

Option 2 · Without the app
Email us from the address you signed up with at support.nutrifit@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my NutriFit account”. We will delete your account and confirm by email within 30 days at most.

Request deletion by email

What gets deleted?

  • Your login (email and password).
  • Your profile: name, date of birth, weight, height, goals and restrictions.
  • Everything you logged: meals, water and weight history.
  • Your profile photo and the messages you sent in “Help & feedback”.
  • Your AI usage counter.

Is anything kept?

NutriFit deletes your data right away. Our providers' technical backups may be kept for a limited time before they are deleted automatically. If you had a subscription, payment records are kept by the store (App Store or Google Play) under their own policies; deleting your account does not cancel your subscription — do that from your store settings.
