FAQ

How do I log what I ate?

In Add you can take a photo, type what you ate (or dictate it with your keyboard's microphone), scan a barcode or search for a food. If you've eaten it before, use Quick log to repeat it with one tap. At night you can also describe your whole day in one sentence.

I logged something by mistake. How do I change or delete it?

On the Today screen, tap the food. From there you can move it to another meal, adjust the grams or delete it.

How accurate are the nutrition values?

They are estimates. We use USDA reference values, and the AI estimates portions from what you describe or photograph. If you know the exact grams, adjust them for better accuracy.

How many times can I use the AI? (fair use)

To keep NutriFit fast and affordable, each account includes up to 60 AI uses per day. Each analyzed photo, smart search, Quick list, message to the assistant and set of suggestions counts as one use. The counter resets at midnight (your phone's time). If you reach the limit, you can keep logging with Quick log or by repeating meals. We may adjust this limit; if we do, we'll let you know in the app and on this page.

What languages is the app in?

English, Spanish and Portuguese. It uses your phone's language, and you can change it in Profile → Language.

Does NutriFit replace my doctor or dietitian?

No. NutriFit is a tool to help you keep track of what you eat. For health decisions, talk to a professional.

How do I delete my account and data?

In the app go to Profile → Delete my account and data. Your account and all your information are deleted. If you can't get into the app, email us at support.nutrifit@gmail.com from the address you signed up with and we'll do it for you. More details in Delete account.
